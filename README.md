This project presents an interactive HR Analytics Dashboard built using Tableau. The dashboard is designed to analyze employee-related data and provide actionable insights for HR decision-making. It focuses on key HR metrics such as job satisfaction, work-life balance, employee retention, promotion trends, and business travel patterns.
Employee Satisfaction Analysis – Understand factors affecting job satisfaction.

Work-Life Balance Insights – Explore correlations between work-life balance and retention.

Promotion Trends – Identify patterns in promotions and possible biases.

Retention Analysis – Discover external/internal factors influencing employee attrition.

Interactive Visualizations – Filter and drill down by department, gender, job role, and more.

Some of the key insights derived from the dashboard:

Job satisfaction is strongly correlated with retention.

Business travel frequency impacts work-life balance.

Promotion opportunities show variance across departments.

Work-life balance plays a crucial role in employee retention.
